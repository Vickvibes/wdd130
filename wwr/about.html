History

Our Story Founded in 1994, we started with just two rafts and a love for the river. Today, our family-run company has guided thousands of guests down everything from easy floats to exciting whitewater. Safety, good times, and great memories—that's what we do. Come join us on the water!

Our company began with a simple goal: help people experience the thrill of nature in a safe, memorable way. Over the years, our team has grown, our guides have become experts, and our adventures have inspired countless travelers.
